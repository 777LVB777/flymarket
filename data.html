Section 5

The data

The real-market test uses historical Polymarket markets. This page explains which markets were chosen and how they are split.

5.1

The markets

4.23MClosed markets scanned
1.44MEnded with a clean yes or no
40,893Qualify for the study

We scanned every closed market on Polymarket, about 4.23 million in total. Of these, 1.44 million ended with a clear YES or NO outcome. A market qualifies for the study if at least $10,000 was traded on it, and only one market is kept per event so that closely related questions are not counted twice. That leaves 40,893 markets.

The data is historical and read only. Polymarket support confirmed in writing that this use is permitted. Market outcomes stay sealed until the test is run.

Fingerprint (SHA-256) of the frozen market list: f3d41123e20ef10b0651b24531e909cb41691e94e1c83df7b0f83a3f2d5975f8

5.2

The split

The training set
Markets that closed before March 1, 2026. There are 16,992 available, and 3,220 are used.
The gap
A seven-day gap separates training from testing, so no market falls on both sides.
The test set
Markets whose decision time falls between March 8 and August 31, 2026. There are 17,944 available, and 8,280 are used, with 15 percent held in reserve.
The benchmark
The frozen training-calibrated price: a forecast based on market price alone, fitted only on the training markets.
The control
The same circuit, trained on outcomes that have been randomly shuffled. It must not beat the benchmark; if it did, the verdict would be null control failed: a pipeline problem, not a finding, and no edge would be reported.
The raw data
Not shared. The public repository holds only fingerprints and summary counts.
5.3

The prices

For each market used, we downloaded its price history: about one price per hour for the three days before the circuit makes its decision. This took 12,015 requests at one request per second. Of the markets fetched, 520 were dropped by rules set in advance, nearly all because no price was recorded early enough, which left exactly the 3,220 training and 8,280 test markets the design calls for.

A final check then confirmed that every price in the data comes from before the decision moment, that the counts and dates match the frozen list, and that the input strengths stay within the range already tested on the brain model. The built data file is identified by its fingerprint below.
