Section 3

The trading test

This page will show how well the circuit predicts markets once the verdict is in. Until then, every number and chart here is placeholder data, included only to show the layout.

The numbers on this page are not results. They are randomly generated placeholders. The simulated-market verdict is calculated only after all simulation jobs finish, around October 27. The real-market test then starts automatically and takes about two weeks. No results are looked at before each verdict.

3.1

The scoring

The circuit is trained on one set of markets and then tested on markets it has never seen. On each test market it picks YES, picks NO, or abstains. A correct pick is a win, a wrong pick is a loss, and an abstention earns and loses nothing. Profit and loss is calculated as if the circuit had bought one share at the market price, before any fees.

The circuit passes only if it beats the best possible forecast that uses the market price alone, and only if it also beats a version of itself trained on shuffled outcomes. Both rules were fixed in advance.
